Your first hero, your first battle

As a viewer, you get your own hero in the streamer's Bannerlord campaign. You choose their class, join battles, and build them up over time. Send the commands below in Twitch chat.

These are the default command names. Check this stream's command list for renamed or disabled commands and its specific costs.

  1. Adopt your hero

    !adopt

    Start here. This creates a hero for you in the campaign. Wait for the confirmation in chat or on the stream overlay before continuing. Most other actions need an adopted hero.

  2. Meet your hero

    !gold !stats

    Use !gold for your hero's gold balance and !stats to check their skills and progression. Hero gold is an in-game resource, separate from Twitch Channel Points.

  3. Choose how you want to fight

    !class archer

    Browse Hero Classes, then replace archer with an available class name. Your class determines your fighting role and equipment requirements. Class changes can cost hero gold; check this stream's settings before changing.

  4. Join your first battle

    !summon !attack

    When the streamer enters an eligible battle, use !summon to fight on their side, or !attack to fight on the enemy side. Your hero fights in the game; you don't directly control their movement. Joining depends on the battle type, your hero's condition, and the stream's rules.

  5. Build up your hero

    !equip !retinue

    As you participate, your hero can gain experience and gold according to the campaign settings. Check !gold before spending: !equip upgrades equipment, while !retinue hires or upgrades troops that can join your hero. Read each command's details for costs and requirements.

  6. Join tournaments and explore rewards

    !tournament

    Use !tournament to join the viewer tournament queue when available. You can also redeem this stream's Channel Point Rewards through Twitch for the actions listed in the guide. Start with your hero and a battle; explore the extra systems as you go.
